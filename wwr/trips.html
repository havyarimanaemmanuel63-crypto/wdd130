Gentle River Float

Enjoy a relaxing float down crystal-clear waters surrounded by scenic forests and wildlife. This calm route is ideal for families, first-timers, and children looking for a safe introduction to rafting.

Canyon Rapids Adventure

Experience an exciting mix of thrilling Class III rapids and stunning red-rock canyon views. Guided by our certified experts, this full-day journey balances high-energy splashes with peaceful swimming holes.

Extreme Gorge Expedition

Conquer steep drops and powerful Class IV–V white water on our most intense two-day expedition. Designed for seasoned thrill-seekers, this trip includes riverside camping and gourmet camp meals.
